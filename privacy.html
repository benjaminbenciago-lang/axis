Privacy Policy

Axis Read and Earn · Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information Axis Read and Earn ("Axis", "we", "the app") collects from people who read or write on the site, and how that information is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: if you create an account, we store your email address, the display name you choose, and the date you signed up.
  • Reading activity: which chapters you have read and your progress in each book, so you can continue where you left off.
  • Comments: any comments you post on a chapter, linked to your account.
  • An anonymous device code: used only to count chapter views, including for readers who are not signed in. This code does not identify you personally.
  • Author information (for writers): if you publish a book, we store the book's title, description, cover image, and chapter content you submit, along with payout details you choose to provide (such as an account name, account number and bank name) so that ad-revenue earnings can be paid to you.
  • Support requests: if you contact us through the Help and Support section, we store your message so we can respond.

Advertising and cookies

Axis shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure how ads perform. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and your choices, at Google's Partner Sites policy, and manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

How we use your information

  • To operate the reading and writing features of Axis (saving your progress, showing your comments, publishing your chapters).
  • To calculate ad-revenue earnings for authors, based on chapter views, and to pay authors their share.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To show relevant ads through Google AdSense.

How your information is stored

Account data, books, chapters, comments and payout details are stored using Supabase, a third-party database service. Reasonable technical measures (such as access controls) are used to protect this data, though no online service can guarantee complete security.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. Your display name and comments are visible to other users of the app. Payout details you provide are visible only to you and to the Axis account owner, for the purpose of sending your earnings. Google AdSense may collect data directly through ads shown on the site, under Google's own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your display name and comments at any time while signed in.
  • You can request that your account and associated data be deleted by contacting us through the Help and Support section in the app.
  • You can control ad personalization through your Google account settings at adssettings.google.com.

Children's privacy

Axis is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach us through the Help and Support section inside the Axis app.
